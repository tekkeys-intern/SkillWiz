Content is not the problem. Knowing is.

Every university has courses, trainers and a placement cell. The harder management problem is knowing whether each student is moving from academic exposure to usable capability, and what should happen next.

Assessed as employableNot yet

55 of every 100Indian graduates are assessed as employable. The other 45 are not, and most universities cannot say which students they are until the final year.

Where universities feel it

  • Students arrive with different starting points but receive uniform preparation.
  • Finishing the curriculum does not create evidence of applied capability.
  • Training, assessment, projects and mentoring run as separate activities with separate vendors.
  • Faculty and T&P teams work from scattered spreadsheets.
  • Entrepreneurship sits apart from employability, though both need the same evidence.

Karm-Patri: the evolving capability record

The Karm-Patri (कर्म-पत्री) is each student's living, evidence-backed record of employability and entrepreneurship. It compounds over the degree, updates as the student grows and can be shared with a recruiter in one click.

Owned by the University. Curated by SkillWiz. Carried by the student for life.

Skills get a student shortlisted. Capabilities get them hired, promoted or funded. A skill is a narrow, task-level ability with a short shelf-life. A capability is the mix of skills, attitudes and habits that lets someone adapt and create value. SkillWiz 2.0 builds and evidences both.

Questions we hear most

How is this different from a placement-training vendor or an LMS?

A training vendor delivers sessions; an LMS delivers content. SkillWiz 2.0 connects baselining, goal-setting, learning, assessment, projects, mentoring and outcomes in one journey and records the evidence in each student's Karm-Patri, so the University sees the change in capability, not just attendance.

What does it cost?

₹2,500 per active student per year for the core platform, excluding GST. Extended services such as proctored examinations, training, labs, EUTP, admissions and UMS are quoted on scope.

Do we have to commit the whole university at once?

No. We recommend a university-specific demonstration followed by an 8–12 week controlled pilot on one cohort, with success measures agreed beforehand and a documented scale, refine or stop decision at the end.

Who owns the student data?

The University. SkillWiz processes data only to deliver the agreed services, aligned with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Parent and recruiter visibility is enabled only where the University allows it and the student consents.

Which students benefit most?

The core cohort is usually 5th and 6th semester students, with second year optional. The journey can be extended to a full first-to-eighth-semester Karm-Patri: Discover, Build, Stretch, Launch.

Does it integrate with our ERP and LMS?

Yes. Single sign-on and integration with SIS / ERP, LMS and T&P systems run through standard APIs or scheduled data exchange, reviewed with your IT team before go-live.

We are a company. How do we reach students?

Share a job description. We map it to competencies, shortlist from the Verified Pool through Karm-Patri evidence, pre-screen with custom assessments and AI interviews, and can train selected students to your role specification before they join.

How long before we go live?

Discovery and design take 2–3 weeks, then the pilot runs 8–12 weeks on one cohort. Onboarding, baseline and dashboards are live from the start of the pilot; the plan follows your academic calendar.

How is student data protected?

Each stakeholder sees only what their role permits. Access logging, proctoring audit trails and data-protection controls are documented and reviewed with your IT team before go-live, and retention is agreed in the MoU.

Is it only for engineering colleges?

No. One Karm-Patri framework is configured per discipline: engineering, science, management, health science and agriculture, not an engineering-only tool.
